## 1. Sprint 4 Goal

The goal of Sprint 4 was to complete the Edujournal project by finalizing the remaining functionality, improving software quality through testing and bug fixing, creating and publishing a GUI-enabled Docker image for the JavaFX application, and preparing the project for final presentation and evaluation.

## 6. What Could Be Improved

Although the sprint was successful, several areas for improvement were identified:

- Test automation could be expanded further to include more end-to-end and integration testing scenarios.
- User feedback could be collected earlier in the development lifecycle to allow additional iterations before the final sprint.
- Docker deployment for graphical desktop applications required additional configuration and troubleshooting, highlighting the importance of investigating deployment requirements earlier in future projects.
- More frequent internal review sessions could help identify usability issues sooner.

These lessons will help improve planning, testing, and deployment activities in future software projects.
